Models: Gaussian exchangeable GEE, clustered by worker, with fixed scenario-variant effects.
Effects are expression minus no-expression, assumed constant across variants within each emotion.
GEE Wald tests are asymptotic. With few workers, p-values and CIs may be unreliable; the CSV flags models with fewer than 30 workers (a diagnostic, not a validity cutoff).
Inference is conditional on the five observed variants, not a random sample of new scenarios.
Bars show equally weighted scenario means, which may differ from pooled rating means or GEE-adjusted estimates under imbalance.
Error bars are pointwise percentile worker-cluster bootstrap 95% CIs, not multiplicity-adjusted; their overlap is not a test of condition differences.
