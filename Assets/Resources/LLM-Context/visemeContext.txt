Task: Analyze the provided audio and output a timed viseme sequence.

Audio duration: <measured duration in seconds>
Expected transcript: <transcript>

Use ONLY these viseme labels:
[EE, ER, IH, AH, OH, AE, W_OO, S_Z, CH_J, F_V, TH,
T_L_D_N, B_M_P, K_G_H_NG, R, sil]

Output format:
[{"time": 0.000, "viseme": "sil"}, ...]

Timing rules:
- Use seconds measured from the beginning of the audio file.
- Each timestamp marks the onset of a viseme. That viseme remains
  active until the next timestamp.
- Determine timing from the audio, not from word count, character
  count, or evenly spaced intervals.
- Preserve the audio's original timing. Do not stretch or compress
  timestamps to match an emotional animation duration.
- Use the transcript to identify words, but use the audio to locate
  their boundaries. Do not assign sounds to words absent from the audio.
- Represent leading silence and audible pauses with "sil".
- Place B_M_P at the lip-closure interval for /b/, /m/, or /p/,
  rather than only at the consonant's release.
- Include the final spoken sounds without truncation.
- If trailing silence exists, place "sil" where speech ends, not
  merely at the audio file's endpoint.
- All timestamps must be nonnegative, strictly increasing, and
  no greater than the supplied audio duration.
- Merge consecutive entries with the same viseme.
- Do not add a final entry solely to make the last timestamp equal
  the audio duration.

Output rules:
- Each object must contain exactly "time" and "viseme".
- Do not invent or substitute labels.
- Output ONLY valid JSON. No preamble or markdown.
